Our Curriculum

A modular, proven approach to building confidence layer by layer, rooted in behavioral psychology and modern pedagogy.

The Three Pillars

Mindset

Overcoming the fear of judgment through cognitive reframing and supportive peer environments.

Mechanics

Vocal projection, body language, pacing, and eye contact fundamentals.

Message

Structuring thoughts logically, storytelling arcs, and persuasive reasoning.

A Typical 6-Week Journey

W1

Icebreakers & Comfort Zones

Establishing a safe space, getting to know peers, and short 30-second impromptu speaking games.

W2

The Art of Storytelling

Understanding narrative arcs. Students share a personal story using descriptive language.

W3

Body Language & Voice

Focusing entirely on non-verbal communication. Posture, gestures, and vocal projection drills.
